OCMT — International Mountaineering Community

Outdoors Club
Mountaineering Team.

OCMT is an international, youth-oriented mountaineering community based in Spain but represented worldwide. Our goal is to be pioneers in creating the next generation of outdoor enthusiasts through education, community, and respect for nature.

What OCMT is

Our Pillars

01

Education / Sustainability

Certified alpinism, mountaineering and canyoning training with EMAM, paired with low-impact, leave-no-trace practices on every route we run.

02

Community

Born at the IE Outdoors Club, now 1,800+ members strong and bound by the mountains. Fellowship over competition, wherever the trail leads.

03

Creative House

Every expedition is captured in photo and film by our in-house creative team, turning summits into micro-documentaries that show the human side of the climb.
